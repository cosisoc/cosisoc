<Chapter title="Overview" teaser="Where does international aid money flow, and how has that changed over the years?" wide>

In the course we had to pick a statistic and turn it into a data visualisation. We chose the flow of international aid. The data comes from the OECD statistic “DAC2A: Aid (ODA) disbursements to countries and regions”. It records how much official development assistance (ODA) was actually paid out, broken down by donor and recipient countries.

We thought about how to show these flows. The visualisation shows which countries send money to which, and how that changes over the years. You can also see the biggest donors and the countries that received the most money.

I did the frontend: how the visualisation looks in the end and how the data can be shown. We cut the project video in Adobe Premiere Pro.
