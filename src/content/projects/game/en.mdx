The Colors We Feel was made during lab week in the winter semester 26/27. There were four of us, three from Interaction Design and one from Product Design, and we had exactly three days. In that time we built a whole world with our own assets, came up with a story and did the sound design and lighting. We developed it in Unity and worked together through GitHub.

I sketched Bob on paper first, then modelled and rigged him in Blender. To do this I controlled Blender with Claude through an MCP server.

</Chapter>

After that I made all the animations: basic movement like idle, walking, sprinting and jumping, facial expressions for the different emotions, and on top of that several dances, sitting down, falling over and being pushed. To bring the street to life, the NPCs have their own animations too, like carrying a bag, playing ball or playing tag.

I also built the two cutscenes at the start: the escape from the sewers and Bob's arrival in the city.

</Chapter>

<Chapter title="Sneak Peek" teaser="A blob without feelings arrives in a city where everyone glows in the colour of their emotions." video="/projects/game/sneak-peek.mp4" label="Highlights from the game">

Every character in the game is a blob, a very soft, squishy creature. Bob lives in the sewers among black blobs who feel nothing. Because he is grey, they bully him. He runs away through the tunnels, gets chased and finally escapes through a manhole cover.

Up there he lands in a glowing cyberpunk street full of blobs. Each one has an orb inside that glows in the colour of its emotion. Only Bob doesn't. That's why the opening is meant to feel overwhelming: it is loud and crowded, Bob is in everyone's way and gets yelled at. This gives him his first emotion, insecurity, and from then on he glows too.

He is only allowed into the green, happy district once he has learned to be happy. So he is sent to an arcade, the first quest, and while playing he gains the emotion joy. In the new district he helps someone pick up the apples they dropped and learns empathy.

</Chapter>

<Chapter title="Working with AI" teaser="Without AI, a game like this in three days would not have been possible. The ideas still had to come from us." wide>

We worked with Claude Desktop, connected directly to Unity and Blender through MCP servers. We generated textures and sounds with tools such as ElevenLabs. This let us get a lot done in very little time.

What AI could not do was the actual design work: the concept, the worldbuilding and the storytelling. How should the opening feel, where does each NPC walk, how do you guide players through the world? We had to decide that ourselves.

The team worked together really well. At times we were worried we wouldn't finish, but in the end we were done on time, without a single all-nighter.

</Chapter>
